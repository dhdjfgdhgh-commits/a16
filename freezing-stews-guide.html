Freezing Stews Guide

Stews are one of the best foods to freeze. Do it safely and they come back tasting just as good as the day you made them.

Kitchen notes

  • Cool within 2 hours
  • Leave 2 cm headspace
  • Label with name and date
  • Best within 3 months

ICool it quickly

Do not leave a big pot to cool on the stove overnight. Divide the stew into shallow containers so it cools fast, and get it into the fridge or freezer within about two hours of cooking. Follow your local food-safety guidance.

IIPack it well

Use freezer-safe containers or flat-frozen bags. Leave about 2 cm of space at the top because liquid expands. Squeeze air out of bags and freeze them flat; they stack neatly and thaw faster.

IIIWhat freezes well, and what doesn’t

Freezes well

  • Meat and bean stews
  • Tomato and stock bases
  • Carrots, onions, celery

Add fresh later

  • Potatoes (can turn grainy)
  • Cream and yoghurt
  • Soft herbs and greens

IVThaw safely

Thaw overnight in the fridge, or use the defrost setting of a microwave if you are eating it straight away. Avoid thawing on the counter at room temperature.

VReheat properly

Reheat gently in a pot with a splash of water or stock, stirring, until it simmers and is piping hot throughout. Reheat only once, and only what you will eat.
