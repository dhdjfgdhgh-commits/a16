About Stewpot

We believe in the long simmer.

Stewpot is an independent home-cooking site about stews, braises and one-pot dinners.

We think a pot simmering on a weeknight is one of the kindest things you can do for yourself and the people you feed. Our guides explain the few techniques that matter, and our tools help you cook from what you have and plan ahead.

We do not sell products, take payment for mentions or use affiliate links. Every guide is cooked in a home kitchen, checked by a second editor and dated.

— The Stewpot kitchen
